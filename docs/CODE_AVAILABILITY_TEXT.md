# Repository-consistent code-availability wording

The standalone implementation of DIAL-Det is publicly available at the project
repository. It includes the role-routed relation encoder, training code,
DIAL-Out and DIAL-Prop contracts, directionally consistent graph construction,
branch-specific value functions, exact and LP-bounded component optimization,
global dynamic programming, bounded-risk and complete-rule calibration
utilities, experiment protocols, executable examples, tests, and
machine-readable values reported in the article. The article and supplementary
source files are not duplicated in the repository; their PDFs are distributed
through the journal. Datasets, third-party detector implementations and weights,
trained article checkpoints, and large raw prediction or private timing
archives are not redistributed and must be obtained or regenerated according
to their original licenses and the documented protocol.
